<h3 class="wp-block-heading">What Factors Affect Website Cost in Toronto?</h3>

<p>Five things drive most of the pricing variation you'll see between Toronto web agencies:</p>

<h4>1. Number of Pages</h4>
<p>A 5-page brochure site (Home, About, Services, Blog, Contact) costs significantly less than a 30-page site with dedicated service pages, location pages, and a blog archive. Every additional page requires design, development, content, and SEO work.</p>

<h4>2. Custom Design vs. Templates</h4>
<p>Template-based websites look generic and rank poorly because dozens of other businesses use the same code. Custom-designed websites are unique to your brand and give Google's algorithm better signals. In a competitive market like Toronto, custom design is the better long-term investment.</p>

<h4>3. SEO Setup</h4>
<p>A website without SEO is invisible on Google. Proper SEO setup — keyword-targeted page titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, schema markup, sitemap, canonical URLs, and internal linking — typically adds $300–$800 to a project if it's not included by default. At Canadian Web Designs, SEO is always included in every package.</p>

<h4>4. Functionality</h4>
<p>Contact forms, booking systems, live chat integrations, e-commerce (Shopify, WooCommerce), member portals, and custom calculators all add cost. Each feature requires development time and testing.</p>

<h4>5. Timeline</h4>
<p>Rush projects (needed in under 2 weeks) typically cost 20–30% more. A standard Toronto website project runs 3–5 weeks for a first draft, with revisions and launch taking 4–6 weeks total.</p>
