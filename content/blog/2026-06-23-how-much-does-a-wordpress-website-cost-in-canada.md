<p>One of the most common questions we hear from Canadian business owners is: <strong>"How much does a WordPress website cost?"</strong> The honest answer is: it depends — but we can give you real numbers based on 500+ Canadian website projects since 2014.</p>

<p>Whether you're a small business in Toronto, a contractor in Calgary, or a healthcare clinic in Vancouver, this guide breaks down every cost involved in building and maintaining a WordPress website in Canada in 2026.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Real Cost of a WordPress Website in Canada (2026)</h2>

<p>WordPress itself is free and open-source software. But building a professional WordPress website that actually ranks on Google and converts visitors into paying customers involves several cost layers. Here's what Canadian businesses typically pay:</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">1. Domain Name: $12–$25/year</h3>

<p>Your domain name (e.g., yourbusiness.ca) typically costs $12–$25 per year through a Canadian registrar like Canada.ca, CIRA, or a third-party registrar. A .ca domain signals Canadian relevance to Google and is recommended for businesses serving Canadian customers.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">2. Web Hosting: $120–$600/year</h3>

<p>WordPress requires a web host to serve your site to visitors. In Canada, there are three common hosting options:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Shared hosting</strong> ($10–$30/month): Suitable for new sites with low traffic. Quality hosts include SiteGround, Bluehost, or Canadian providers like A2 Hosting Canada.</li>
  <li><strong>Managed WordPress hosting</strong> ($25–$80/month): Faster, more secure, and optimized for WordPress. WP Engine, Kinsta, and Flywheel are popular choices. This is what CWD recommends for most Canadian businesses.</li>
  <li><strong>VPS or dedicated hosting</strong> ($80–$300/month): For high-traffic sites or ecommerce stores. Typically unnecessary for most small businesses.</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">3. WordPress Theme: $0–$200 (one-time)</h3>

<p>Free WordPress themes exist, but most professional agencies build custom designs or use premium themes as a starting point. Premium themes from marketplaces like ThemeForest cost $50–$200 one-time. However, at Canadian Web Designs, we build fully custom designs for every client — no templates — because templated sites limit your SEO and brand differentiation.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">4. Plugins: $0–$500/year</h3>

<p>WordPress plugins extend functionality. Essential plugins for a Canadian business site include:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>SEO plugin</strong> (Yoast SEO or Rank Math): Free to $99/year</li>
  <li><strong>Security plugin</strong> (Wordfence): Free to $119/year</li>
  <li><strong>Backup plugin</strong> (UpdraftPlus): Free to $70/year</li>
  <li><strong>Forms plugin</strong> (Gravity Forms): $59/year</li>
  <li><strong>Page builder</strong> (Elementor Pro): $99/year</li>
</ul>

<p>Plan for $100–$300/year in plugin costs for a well-functioning site.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">5. Professional Design & Development: $1,499–$10,000+</h3>

<p>This is where the biggest cost variation occurs. Options for Canadian businesses:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>DIY with a template</strong>: $0–$500 in tools, but significant time investment and typically weak SEO performance</li>
  <li><strong>Freelancer</strong>: $800–$3,000 depending on experience and scope. Quality varies widely; ask for references and ranked examples</li>
  <li><strong>Professional agency (like CWD)</strong>: $1,499–$5,000+ for a complete custom website. This includes custom design, mobile optimization, on-page SEO, Google Analytics setup, and post-launch support</li>
  <li><strong>Enterprise agency</strong>: $10,000–$50,000+ for complex multi-site builds, e-commerce platforms, or large-scale web applications</li>
</ul>

<p>At Canadian Web Designs, our WordPress websites start at <strong>$1,499 for a 5-page custom site</strong>, scaling to $3,499+ for growth packages with ongoing SEO. We've built 500+ WordPress sites for Canadian businesses since 2014.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Ongoing WordPress Website Costs in Canada</h2>

<p>Building the site is only part of the investment. Running a professional WordPress website requires ongoing costs that many Canadian business owners don't anticipate:</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">WordPress Maintenance: $99–$349/month</h3>

<p>WordPress requires regular updates — the core software, themes, and plugins need to be kept current to maintain security and performance. A professional <a href="/services/website-maintenance">website maintenance plan</a> in Canada typically costs $99–$349/month and includes:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Core WordPress and plugin updates</li>
  <li>Daily backups (stored offsite)</li>
  <li>24/7 uptime monitoring</li>
  <li>Malware scanning and removal</li>
  <li>Speed optimization and Core Web Vitals monitoring</li>
  <li>Monthly performance reports</li>
</ul>

<p>Skipping maintenance is one of the most common (and costly) mistakes Canadian business owners make. A hacked or broken WordPress site costs far more to repair than prevention.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">SEO Services: $499–$2,500+/month</h3>

<p>A WordPress website that no one can find is a wasted investment. <a href="/services/seo">Professional SEO services in Canada</a> ensure your site ranks for the keywords your customers are searching. Most Canadian small businesses invest $499–$999/month in ongoing local SEO to maintain and improve their Google rankings.</p>
