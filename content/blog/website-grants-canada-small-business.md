<p><strong>Short answer:</strong> the Canada Digital Adoption Program (CDAP), the grant most small businesses used to pay for a website, is closed. It ended on March 31, 2025, and no new federal website grant has replaced it. What is left in 2026 is mostly <strong>loans and advice</strong>: BDC's LIFT program for larger businesses, the Canada Small Business Financing Program through your bank, Futurpreneur for young founders, and local business centres that run occasional grant intakes.</p>

<p>Below is what each one covers, who qualifies, and what to do if none of them fit. We checked every program against its official page in November 2026.</p>

<h2>What happened to CDAP</h2>

<p>CDAP launched in 2021 with two streams. <strong>Grow Your Business Online</strong> gave micro-grants of up to $2,400 to help small businesses sell and market online. <strong>Boost Your Business Technology</strong> paid up to $15,000 toward a digital adoption plan and gave access to a 0% BDC loan of up to $100,000.</p>

<p>Demand was high and both streams closed to new applications in 2024. BDC stopped taking Boost Your Business Technology applications on February 19, 2024. Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED) lists the program as concluded on March 31, 2025.</p>

<p>If you are reading an agency page that still says "get your website paid for by CDAP", it is out of date.</p>

<h3>BDC LIFT: the official CDAP successor, but not for most small shops</h3>

<p>BDC's <a href="https://www.bdc.ca/en/solutions/lift" target="_blank" rel="noopener">LIFT program</a> is the offer BDC now points former CDAP applicants to. It combines consulting with financing of $25,000 to $2 million, with up to 12 months of postponed capital payments and up to 6 years to repay.</p>

<p>The catch: the digital stream requires at least $1 million in annual revenue, a profitable business and a plan for AI or digital adoption. It finances things like data platforms, cybersecurity, ERP and CRM systems and AI projects. A website or online store is not on its list. If you are a growing company rolling out a CRM with a new website as part of it, ask BDC. If you are a five-person shop that needs a website, it is not built for you.</p>

<h3>Canada Small Business Financing Program</h3>

<p>The <a href="https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-small-business-financing-program/en" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CSBFP</a> is not a grant. The government shares the risk with your bank so it is easier to get a loan. ISED says it can finance working capital, intellectual property, renovations and equipment. Ask your bank whether a website, online store or software project fits the loan you are applying for.</p>

<h3>Futurpreneur</h3>

<p><a href="https://www.futurpreneur.ca/en/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Futurpreneur</a> lends up to $75,000 to young entrepreneurs who have been in business for up to 24 months, and pairs each one with a mentor. A website is a normal startup cost, so it can be part of your business plan and budget.</p>

<h3>Digital Main Street and local business centres</h3>

<p>Ontario's Digital Main Street has funded thousands of main street businesses through grants and its free Digital Service Squad. Grant trackers list its grants as closed in 2026, and we could not confirm a new intake. Check <a href="https://digitalmainstreet.ca/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">digitalmainstreet.ca</a> directly before you plan around it.</p>

<p>Your local <strong>Small Business Enterprise Centre</strong> (Toronto, Mississauga, Brampton and most Ontario cities have one) is the best place to hear about new provincial and city programs first. Their advice is free.</p>

<h2>Watch out for "grant" offers from agencies</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>"Government-funded website" with no program name.</strong> Ask which program, and check it on a .gc.ca, .ontario.ca or bdc.ca page.</li>
<li><strong>Paying an agency to "apply for you".</strong> Federal and provincial programs do not require a paid consultant to apply.</li>
<li><strong>Prices raised to match the grant.</strong> A website that cost $1,500 before a grant should not cost $2,400 because a $2,400 grant exists.</li>
</ul>
