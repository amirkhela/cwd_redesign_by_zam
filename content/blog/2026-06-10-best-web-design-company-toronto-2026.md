<h3 class="wp-block-heading">What Makes a Great Toronto Web Design Company?</h3>

<p>The bar for "web design" is surprisingly low. Anyone with a Wix account can call themselves a web designer. A truly great Toronto web design company does something different: they build websites that rank on Google, load fast, and convert visitors into real paying customers. That requires three things most agencies skip — deep SEO knowledge, conversion-focused design, and honest communication.</p>

<p>Here's what to look for:</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">1. Verified Google Reviews (Not Just Testimonials on Their Own Site)</h3>

<p>Testimonials on an agency's website are easy to fake. Google reviews aren't — they require a real Google account and are harder to remove. Before hiring any Toronto web design company, check their Google Business Profile. Look for:</p>

<ul>
  <li>50+ reviews (anything fewer for a company in business 3+ years is a red flag)</li>
  <li>Recent reviews (ongoing activity, not a burst from three years ago)</li>
  <li>Detailed reviews that mention specific results, not just "great to work with"</li>
  <li>Owner responses to negative reviews (shows professionalism)</li>
</ul>

<p>Canadian Web Designs has 180+ five-star Google reviews from verified Toronto clients. That's the kind of track record that's impossible to fake.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">2. A Portfolio With Real Results — Not Just Pretty Screenshots</h3>

<p>Any agency can show you beautiful screenshots. What matters is whether those websites actually perform. Ask specifically:</p>

<ul>

  <li>What's the bounce rate? What's the conversion rate?</li>
  <li>Can I speak to any of your Toronto clients directly?</li>
</ul>

<p>A good Toronto web design company will answer these questions without hesitation. If they get defensive or vague, that's a red flag. <h3 class="wp-block-heading">4. Built-in SEO — Not an Add-On</h3>

<p>In Toronto's competitive market, a beautiful website with no SEO is invisible. Every professional web design company should include, at minimum:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Keyword-optimized page titles and meta descriptions</li>
  <li>Proper H1/H2 heading structure</li>
  <li>Image alt text on every image</li>
  <li>Google Analytics and Search Console setup</li>
  <li>A sitemap submitted to Google</li>
  <li>Mobile-first responsive design</li>
  <li>Core Web Vitals passing scores (90+ PageSpeed)</li>
</ul>

<p>If a Toronto web designer quotes you a website without mentioning any of these, you're likely buying a pretty but invisible site. Our <a href="/services/seo">SEO services in Toronto</a> are built into every project from day one.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Red Flags to Avoid When Hiring a Toronto Web Designer</h3>

<p>Watch out for these common warning signs:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Template sites sold as "custom"</strong> — ask to see the CMS backend and whether the design is based on a purchased theme.</li>
  <li><strong>No physical Toronto presence</strong> — offshore agencies often list a Toronto address with no real team here. Ask for a video call with the actual developers.</li>
  <li><strong>Long lock-in contracts</strong> — a confident agency doesn't need a 12-month commitment. Month-to-month arrangements protect you.</li>
  <li><strong>No guarantee of any kind</strong> — if they won't stand behind their work, why should you pay for it?</li>
  <li><strong>Can't rank their own site</strong> — if a Toronto SEO company doesn't show up on Google for "web design toronto," why would you trust them to rank yours?</li>
</ul>
