<h2 class="wp-block-heading">How Google Business Profile Optimization Works for Surrey Businesses</h2>

<p>Google Business Profile (GBP) is the single most powerful free tool available to local businesses — and the most commonly misused. When a Surrey resident searches for a service on their phone, the first three results they see are almost always the Google Maps "local pack": three businesses displayed with a map, star ratings, and a phone number.</p>

<p>Getting into that local pack requires a properly optimized GBP. Here is what that means in practice:</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Complete Every Section of Your Profile</h3>

<p>Business name, address, phone, website, hours, and category are the basics — but they're not enough. You need a detailed business description that naturally includes keywords like "Surrey" and your service type. You need to add every relevant secondary category Google offers. You need to upload high-quality photos of your work, your location, and your team.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Build and Respond to Reviews</h3>

<p>Google's local ranking algorithm places heavy weight on review volume, recency, and your response rate. A Surrey business with 40 recent reviews and consistent owner responses will outrank a competitor with 200 old reviews and no engagement. We help Surrey clients build a systematic review generation process that produces a steady flow of authentic, five-star feedback.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Use GBP Posts and Q&A Consistently</h3>

<p>Google rewards active profiles. Weekly posts about promotions, completed projects, or local community involvement signal to Google that your business is active and relevant. The Q&A section allows you to pre-populate common questions with keyword-rich answers that appear directly on your listing.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Ensure NAP Consistency Across the Web</h3>

<p>Your business Name, Address, and Phone number must be identical everywhere it appears online — your website, GBP, Yelp, Yellow Pages, local directories, and social media. Inconsistencies confuse Google and suppress your local rankings. This is a technical cleanup task that most business owners overlook entirely.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Questions to Ask Before Hiring a Surrey SEO Agency</h2>

<p>Not all SEO agencies deliver what they promise. Before signing a contract with any <strong>Surrey SEO company</strong>, ask these questions — and pay close attention to the quality of the answers.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">1. Can you show me real results from real Surrey or BC clients?</h3>

<p>Any legitimate SEO agency should be able to demonstrate ranking improvements, traffic growth, and lead generation outcomes for past clients. Vague testimonials and generic case studies are not sufficient — ask for specific keyword rankings and Google Analytics data.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">2. What SEO practices do you use — white hat or black hat?</h3>

<p>Black-hat tactics like buying links, keyword stuffing, or cloaking can produce short-term ranking gains followed by Google penalties that devastate your business. Insist on white-hat, Google-compliant methods only. Ask specifically how the agency builds links and what their content strategy looks like.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">3. How do you report on results, and how often?</h3>

<p>Monthly reporting with clear metrics — keyword rankings, organic traffic, conversion actions — is the minimum standard. You should always know exactly what work was done and what results it produced. Agencies that resist transparency or provide vague reports are hiding poor performance.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">4. Do you offer long-term contracts with no exit clause?</h3>

<p>SEO takes time, but you should never be locked into a contract with no recourse if the agency underperforms. Look for agencies that offer month-to-month arrangements or clear performance benchmarks built into the agreement.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">5. Do you have experience with Surrey's specific neighbourhoods and market?</h3>

<p>Generic SEO knowledge is not the same as local market knowledge. An agency that understands the demographic differences between Cloverdale and South Surrey, or the search behaviour differences between Newton and Guildford, will build a far more effective strategy than one applying a generic template.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">6. Who will actually be working on my account?</h3>

<p>Some agencies sell you on senior strategists and then hand your account to junior staff or offshore teams. Confirm who will be doing the work, how frequently they will communicate with you, and who your primary point of contact will be.</p>
